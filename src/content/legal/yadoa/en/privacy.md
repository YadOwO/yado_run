Yado Asset ("the App") is a bookkeeping app developed and operated by the independent developer **YU JUNJIE** ("we", "us"). Your ledger is private, so this policy explains in plain language what we collect, why, where it is stored, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and how you can control it.

**Please pay particular attention to the parts in bold, especially those about sensitive information and sharing with third parties.** If you have any questions, contact us using the details at the end.

## The short version

- **You can keep books without signing in, and your ledger stays on your phone by default.**
- Your ledger is uploaded to our servers only if you turn on **Sync**. Content is sent to an AI model only when you use **AI bookkeeping**.
- Speech is converted to text on your device. **We never receive or store your voice recordings.**
- No ads, no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, no tracking. **We do not sell or share your personal information.**
- You can export or delete your data at any time, and delete your account from within the App.

## 1. What we collect and why

The App has core features and optional features. Core features (manual entries, accounts, categories, charts, reminders) run entirely on your device, need no account, and send no ledger content to us. Below is what each optional feature handles.

### Account

Sync, AI bookkeeping, AI points and feedback require an account. You can choose:

- **Sign in with Apple**: we receive an identifier from Apple and, if you allow it on Apple's screen, your name and email. If you choose "Hide My Email", we receive a relay address generated by Apple.
- **Username and password**: we collect the username and password you set. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash.

After you sign in, a random account ID is created so our services can tell users apart. You may also set a **nickname** and **profile photo** (both optional). **A profile photo can be viewed by anyone who has its link, so please don't use a photo you want to keep private.**

We run our own sign-in service; no other company's account system is involved (except Apple, which verifies your identity when you use Sign in with Apple).

### Sync (off by default)

When you turn it on, we upload your ledger so it can sync across your devices and be restored on a new one. This includes:

- **Accounts**: name, type, notes, **last four digits of a card** (if you entered them), currency, opening balance;
- **Transactions**: amount, type, category, date, notes, and the accounts involved;
- **Categories, recurring rules** and your default account setting;
- A **random device identifier** for each device (generated by the App; not a serial number or advertising identifier) and sync timestamps.

**This data is encrypted in transit, but it is not end-to-end encrypted on our servers, which means we are technically able to access it.** We use it only to provide sync and will not view, analyze or use it for anything else, except to investigate a problem you report to us or where the law requires.

You can turn Sync off or "Delete cloud data" in Settings at any time.

### AI bookkeeping (requires an account)

You can speak, type or send a picture (from your photo library, the camera or the clipboard) and the AI will record, edit or look up entries for you. To do this, the App sends the following to our AI service, which then passes it to a third-party large language model provider for processing:

- The **text** of what you said or typed;
- **Images** you send (e.g. receipts, bill screenshots);
- Your **account list** (name, type, balance, whether inactive) and **category list**, so the AI picks the right ones;
- The context of the conversation, including **transactions** and **recurring rules** the AI looked up to answer you;
- The current time, time zone and App language, so the AI understands phrases like "yesterday".

**We do not send your account ID to the model provider.** After a request is processed, our AI service **does not keep** your text, images or the AI's replies; it keeps only billing records (see "AI points" below). Your AI chat history is stored only on your device and **deleted automatically after 90 days**.

**We will ask for your explicit permission before you first use AI bookkeeping.** You can choose never to use AI features; everything else still works.

### AI points and daily check-in

AI bookkeeping uses points. New users receive free points, and you can check in daily for more. We keep your point balance, a record of each grant and use (time, feature, model tier, points), your check-in dates, and an **irreversible fingerprint** of each AI request (used to avoid charging twice for the same request; it cannot be turned back into what you said).

### Feedback

When you send feedback in the App, we receive your message plus the App version, OS version, device model and App language, so we can find the problem and reply.

### Server logs

To keep our services secure and reliable, troubleshoot problems and meet legal log-retention requirements, our servers record access logs: **IP address, time, the endpoint requested, the result and duration**, and for some services your account ID and device identifier. **Logs do not contain your ledger, chat text or images.**

### Device permissions

The App asks for a permission only when you use the related feature. You can turn any of them off in iOS Settings.

| Permission | Used for | If you decline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Microphone | Hold to talk for voice bookkeeping | You can type instead |
| Speech Recognition | Turning what you say into text | Same as above |
| Camera | Taking a photo of a receipt for the AI | You can pick from your library instead |
| Notifications | Reminders at the times you choose | No reminders |

**About speech recognition:** speech is converted to text by iOS's built-in speech recognition. On recent devices and iOS versions it runs entirely on your device. On some devices or versions without on-device support, iOS may send the audio to Apple, subject to Apple's privacy policy. **In either case, the audio is never sent to us.**

**About photos:** the App uses the system photo picker, so it can read only the photos you select and cannot see your library. No photo library permission is needed.

### What we don't do

- We don't collect your contacts, location, health data, clipboard (unless you paste) or advertising identifier (IDFA);
- We don't track you across apps or websites, build profiles or show personalized ads;
- We don't use any third-party analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs;
- **We don't sell your personal information.**

## 2. Sensitive information

**Account balances, card digits and transaction history are financial information**, and receipts or bill images you send to the AI may contain it too.

- By default this information stays on your device;
- It leaves your device only if you **turn on Sync** or **use AI bookkeeping**, and we explain this and ask for your consent first;
- If it were exposed, others could learn about your finances. We protect it as described below and keep processing to a minimum.

**The App is not directed to children under 14.**

## 3. Sharing

### Third parties we provide data to

We provide your information to third parties only in the cases below. See the [Third-Party Sharing List](/legal/yadoa/sharing/) for each recipient's name, what they receive and why.

- **AI bookkeeping**: when you use AI bookkeeping, our server sends the related content to large language model providers (currently Alibaba Cloud Model Studio, DeepSeek and Volcano Engine Ark) so they can understand your request and produce entries. **Your account ID is never included. We will ask for your explicit permission before you first use AI bookkeeping.** Providers process the data under their own privacy policies and terms.
- **Apple**: Apple verifies your identity when you use Sign in with Apple, and on devices without on-device recognition, iOS sends speech to Apple for recognition. iOS handles this directly; we are not involved.

**If we add a new recipient, we will update the list and ask for your permission again before you next use the related feature.**

### Service providers

Our servers, database, sign-in service and profile photo storage run on **Alibaba Cloud** in Guangzhou, China. Alibaba Cloud provides infrastructure on our instructions and does not use your information for its own purposes.

### Third-party SDKs

The App includes only three open-source libraries (Logto Swift SDK, JOSESwift, KeychainAccess) for signing in and storing sign-in tokens securely. They run on your device and **send no data to any third party**; see the list for details. The App has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or push notification SDKs.

The sign-in page opens in an in-app browser and uses cookies only to keep the sign-in session.

### Disclosure and transfer

We do not disclose your personal information unless you consent separately or the law, a court or a government authority lawfully requires it. If the App comes to be operated by someone else through a merger or transfer, we will tell you who the new operator is and how to contact them, and require them to keep to this policy.

## 4. Where and how long we store data

**Location: your information is stored in the People's Republic of China** (Alibaba Cloud, Guangzhou). If you use the App outside China, your information is transferred to and processed in China, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

**Retention:** we keep information only as long as needed:

| Information | Kept for |
| --- | --- |
| Ledger and AI chat history on your device | On your device, under your control; AI chat history is deleted after 90 days |
| Sync data | Until you delete cloud data or your account; deleted records are purged from the server within 90 days |
| Account info, nickname, profile photo | Until you delete your account |
| AI points and check-ins | Until you delete your account |
| Feedback | Until you delete your account |
| Server logs | 6 months |

After that, we delete or anonymize the information. Database backups are overwritten by newer backups at the end of each backup cycle.

## 5. Security

- All network traffic uses HTTPS (TLS);
- Sign-in tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain, and passwords are stored only as one-way hashes;
- Servers expose only the ports they need, the database is not reachable from the internet, and only the developer has administrative access;
- Each service can access only its own data; the AI service cannot read your synced ledger;
- We minimize data: for example, the model provider never receives your account ID, and the AI service does not keep conversation content.

No system is perfectly secure, and **Sync data is not currently end-to-end encrypted**. If a security incident affects your personal information, we will tell you promptly, as required by law, through the App or by email, including what happened, its possible impact, what we have done and what you can do.

## 6. Your choices and rights

- **Access and copy**: your ledger is always visible in the App, and you can **export a backup file** from Settings for a complete copy.
- **Correct**: edit entries, accounts, nickname and profile photo directly in the App.
- **Delete**: delete any entry, or erase device data or cloud data in Settings. After erasing device data, the App keeps an undo copy on the device for 7 days, then deletes it automatically.
- **Withdraw consent**: turn off Sync, stop using AI bookkeeping, or revoke permissions in iOS Settings, at any time. This doesn't affect processing done before.
- **Delete your account**: in the App's Settings. We then delete, or anonymize, your account information, sync data, AI points and check-ins, feedback and profile photo. **This cannot be undone, so export anything you want to keep first.** The ledger on your device is not affected unless you choose to erase it too.
- **Ask and complain**: contact us for an explanation of this policy or how we handle your data.

For anything you can't do in the App, contact us using the details below. We will respond within **15 business days** after verifying your identity.

## 7. Children

**The App is not directed to children under 14**, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 14, please don't use features that require personal information, such as sign-in, Sync and AI. If you are between 14 and 18, please use the App with your parent's or guardian's permission. If we learn that we have collected information from a child without the required consent, we will delete it. Parents or guardians can contact us with any concerns.

## 8. Additional information for users in the United States

- **Categories of personal information** we collect are described in section 1: identifiers (account ID, device identifier, IP address), personal information you provide (name, email, username), financial information (your ledger, if you use Sync or AI), photos you send to the AI, feedback content and device information.
- **We do not sell or "share" personal information** for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we have not done so in the past 12 months. We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you.
- **Your rights**: depending on your state, you may have the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information, and to obtain a portable copy. You can exercise most of these in the App, or by emailing us. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask you to verify your identity. **We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.**
- **Do Not Track**: the App does not track you across third-party websites or apps, so Do Not Track signals don't change how it works.
- **Data location**: your information is stored and processed in China, as described in section 4.
